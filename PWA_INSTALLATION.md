# Mohamed Mustafa Portfolio - PWA Installation Guide

## ✨ Features
This portfolio is now a Progressive Web App (PWA) that can be installed on both desktop and mobile devices.

## 📱 Installation on Mobile Devices

### iOS (iPhone/iPad)
1. Open the portfolio in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** button (arrow pointing up)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add** to confirm
5. The app will now appear on your home screen with the app icon

### Android
1. Open the portfolio in Chrome browser
2. Tap the **menu** button (three dots) at the top right
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**
4. Confirm the installation
5. The app will now appear on your home screen with the app icon

## 🖥️ Installation on Desktop

### Windows
1. Open the portfolio in Chrome or Edge
2. Click the **Install** button that appears in the address bar (or click the menu → **Install app**)
3. Follow the installation wizard
4. The app will be installed and you can launch it from:
   - Desktop shortcut
   - Start menu
   - Windows app list

### macOS
1. Open the portfolio in Chrome
2. Click the **Install** button in the address bar
3. Click **Install** to confirm
4. The app will be added to your Applications folder
5. You can launch it like any other macOS app

### Linux
1. Open the portfolio in Chrome
2. Click the **Install** button in the address bar
3. Follow the installation steps
4. The app will be available in your applications menu

## 🔄 Offline Support
The app includes offline support - previously visited pages will be cached and available even without internet connection.

## 🚀 Benefits
- ✅ No need to visit the website URL
- ✅ Full-screen experience like a native app
- ✅ Quick access from home screen or app drawer
- ✅ Works offline for cached content
- ✅ App icon and splash screen on launch
- ✅ Install directly from browser without app store
